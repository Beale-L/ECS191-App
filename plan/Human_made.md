##Yelp Clone

#Stage 1:

Create a iphone app that will help users check ratings on specific locations.
The general UI should be relatively simple, include things such as a navagation bar. 
The main interactive page should be a grid of 'maps' that are uploaded by users. 

These maps are a collection of their favorite locations under a gerneral tag such as 'parks' or 'chinese food'.
When you click on a collection, you are brought to a map that shows off the locations and if you click on it,
a pop containing information on the location shows up.

A user may like, dislike, favorite, or comment under these collections. 

Sign up/sign in's should only be required for users that want to upload or like/interact with a collection.

Exit criteria, make sure the landing page is functional. That means you can click the collections and see any destination.
