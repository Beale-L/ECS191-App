#Part 1: Proposal

##1: The App
This project will be recreating a Yelp app. 
I'm choosing to make a Yelp clone because I find myself trying to find good food places near me but
yelp will give me a review of a resturant across town. It also has a great 'collection' tab but is 
difficult to know where every resturant is without having to click on every individual business.

##2: The fundamental problem
Yelp focueses on allowing people to review businesses and be able to determine whether it is 'worth it'
to visit. It removes some of the risk of visiting a new place.

##3: What I'll keep
The Yelp review system is good, it is relatively simple to understand and allows for people to see how
a business actually is day to day.
I will also be keeping the collection feature where users can create their own set of businesses/resturant
and share it as a group.

##4: What I'll change or remove
I plan on emphasising the collection function, being able to follow critics you connect with, and making
the ui/ux more visual to emphasis the location of the business alongside the reviews. I also plan on 
changing the review process to make the rating system be more fair. This is because most reviews are either
"I loved it!! 5 Stars" or "Food was disgusting...worst place ever. 1 star" and rarely in between.
